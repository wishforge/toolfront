About ToolFront-Scanner

User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0-compatible · ToolFront-Scanner/0.3 (+https://toolfront.dev/bot)

Who we are

ToolFront is a website agent-readiness scanner operated by an independent developer (toolfront.dev). Our scanner, ToolFront-Scanner, fetches a handful of publicly accessible files from domains that a visitor explicitly submits for a free scan. We are not a crawler: we never follow links, never index content, and never scan a domain nobody asked us to scan.

What we request

For each submitted domain, at most these five public paths, once per scan:

  • / (homepage HTML)
  • /robots.txt
  • /llms.txt
  • /sitemap.xml
  • /openapi.json

Requests come from Cloudflare Workers (datacenter IPs) with the User-Agent shown above. We do not execute JavaScript, set cookies, or submit any forms.

How often

Only when a scan is requested. Results are cached for up to 24 hours; repeat scans of the same domain within that window do not touch your server.

How to opt out

Add this to your robots.txt — our scanner honors an explicit ban on its own name before any other request is scored:

User-agent: ToolFront-Scanner
Disallow: /

A wildcard User-agent: * block is not treated as an opt-out for the one-shot readiness probe (we fetch 5 public files, not crawl). If you want us fully out regardless, use the rule above, or email privacy@toolfront.dev.

Seeing a "blocked" result?

If your bot protection (e.g. Cloudflare challenge pages) intercepts our scanner, the report says so honestly instead of scoring your site as a failure. The same wall usually stops AI agents — if that is not your intent, consider allowing verified automated readers.

Contact

Questions or block requests: privacy@toolfront.dev. See also our privacy policy.

关于 ToolFront-Scanner

User-Agent：Mozilla/5.0-compatible · ToolFront-Scanner/0.3 (+https://toolfront.dev/bot)

我们是谁

ToolFront 是一个网站 Agent 就绪度扫描器（toolfront.dev）。我们的爬虫 ToolFront-Scanner 只在访客主动提交某个域名进行免费扫描时，抓取该域名的少量公开可访问文件。我们不是爬虫：不追踪链接、不索引内容、绝不扫描没有人提交过的域名。

我们请求什么

对每个被提交的域名，每次扫描最多请求以下 5 个公开路径：

  • /（首页 HTML）
  • /robots.txt
  • /llms.txt
  • /sitemap.xml
  • /openapi.json

请求来自 Cloudflare Workers（数据中心 IP），User-Agent 如上所示。我们不执行 JavaScript、不设置 Cookie、不提交任何表单。

频率

仅在有扫描请求时访问。扫描结果最长缓存 24 小时；缓存窗口内对同一域名的重复扫描不会触达你的服务器。

如何退出

在 robots.txt 中加入以下规则——我们的扫描器在执行任何其他请求前，会优先遵守针对自身名称的明确禁令：

User-agent: ToolFront-Scanner
Disallow: /

通配符 User-agent: * 的封禁不会被视为对本工具的退出（我们只抓取 5 个公开文件，不进行爬取）。如需完全屏蔽，请使用上面的规则，或发邮件至 privacy@toolfront.dev。

看到「被拦截」结果？

如果你的 bot 防护（如 Cloudflare 挑战页）拦截了我们的扫描器，报告会如实说明，而不是把你的站点判为不及格。同一道墙通常也会挡住 AI agent——如果这不是你的本意，建议放行经验证的自动化读取方。

联系方式

疑问或屏蔽请求：privacy@toolfront.dev。另见隐私政策。
