Security & Vulnerability Disclosure

Version 1.0 · toolfront.dev

How to report

Email security@toolfront.dev. Please include: a description of the issue, steps to reproduce (or a PoC), the affected URL or endpoint, and an estimate of impact. You may encrypt your report — our PGP key is available on request.

Our commitment

  • Acknowledgement within 72 hours of receiving your report.
  • Triage within 7 days — we will tell you whether we accept it and our planned timeline.
  • No legal action against good-faith research that respects the scope below.
  • Credit in our release notes if you wish (opt-in).

Scope

In scope: toolfront.dev and its API endpoints (/api/scan, /api/waitlist, /api/resend, /confirm, /unsubscribe).
Out of scope: volumetric denial-of-service, social engineering, physical attacks, spam, automated scanner output without a demonstrated vulnerability, issues on third-party services (report those to Cloudflare or Resend).

Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to comply with this policy during your research, we will consider your activity authorized, will not initiate legal action, and will work with you to understand and resolve the issue quickly.

安全与漏洞披露政策

版本 1.0 · toolfront.dev

如何报告

请发送邮件至 security@toolfront.dev，内容请包含：问题描述、复现步骤（或概念验证）、受影响的 URL 或端点、以及影响评估。如需加密报告，可来信索取我们的 PGP 公钥。

我们的承诺

  • 收到报告后 72 小时内确认。
  • 7 天内完成分诊——告知你是否受理以及计划的修复时间表。
  • 对遵守下述范围、出于善意的研究者不采取法律行动。
  • 经你同意后，在发布说明中致谢。

范围

范围内：toolfront.dev 及其 API 端点（/api/scan、/api/waitlist、/api/resend、/confirm、/unsubscribe）。
范围外：容量型拒绝服务、社会工程、物理攻击、垃圾邮件、未演示出实际漏洞的扫描器原始输出、以及第三方服务上的问题（请报告给 Cloudflare 或 Resend）。

安全港

如果你在研究过程中善意遵守本政策，我们将视你的行为为已获授权，不会对其提起法律诉讼，并将与你合作快速理解与解决问题。
