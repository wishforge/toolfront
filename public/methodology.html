Why our scores may differ from other scanners

Scores are not comparable across scanners — each tool measures a different slice of "agent readiness", usually with different weights and grade bands.

On top of the dimensions discovery-led scanners check, we additionally measure what agents need to operate, not just find you:

  • Content freshness — dateModified / Last-Modified signals, so an agent can tell how current your content is
  • Machine-readable tool surfaces — OpenAPI / structured endpoints an agent can call directly
  • API error responses — what an agent sees when a call fails
  • WebMCP tool registration — when present

Extra dimensions mean our denominator differs, and a lower number here does not mean a worse site — it means more things were checked. Every point traces to a concrete check listed in the report.
