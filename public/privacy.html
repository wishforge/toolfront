Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective date: September 2, 2026 · toolfront.dev

This policy covers toolfront.dev (the AI-readiness scanner) and monitor.toolfront.dev (the Monitor account system), operated by the same controller under the same commitments below.

Who we are (the controller)

ToolFront is operated by David, an independent developer located in Shanghai, China (“ToolFront”, “we”). Controller contact: privacy@toolfront.dev.

What we collect

  • Email address — only when you voluntarily join the waitlist or request a tool blueprint.
  • Domain you scanned — the domain name entered into the scanner, associated with your waitlist request if provided.
  • IP address and timestamps — collected for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and (for waitlist signups) as your consent record under GDPR Art. 7.
  • Language preference — stored in your browser's localStorage (key tf-lang) to remember your language choice. This is strictly functional storage; no cookies or third-party trackers are used.
  • Browser-local comparison data — your fix-list checkboxes and a convenience copy of your latest result (keys tf-fix:* and tf-last:*) are stored in your browser's localStorage to power the "since your last scan" comparison and remember progress. This copy never leaves your browser and can be cleared in browser settings. (The authoritative record of a scan is covered under Public scan history below.)
  • Public scan history — when a scan of a domain succeeds, a record of that scan — domain, score, grade, per-check statuses, scoring version and timestamp — is stored server-side and is publicly visible on the report page and in rankings, so scores can be compared over time. A scan fetches only publicly reachable pages, the way a crawler does: no credentials are used and forms are never submitted. Sites that opt out in robots.txt are never scanned. IP addresses are used only for rate limiting and are never stored with a scan record.
  • Aggregate usage statistics — counts of product events (e.g., signups, email confirmations, logins, monitors added) and their coarse outcome, used to understand the signup funnel and improve the product. These counters cannot identify you: they contain no email address, no domain, no IP address, and no language. Stored by our host in Cloudflare Analytics Engine for 90 days.
  • Monitor account data — if you create a Monitor account (monitor.toolfront.dev): your email address and password (stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash — never readable), the domains you choose to monitor, login timestamps and IP addresses (for rate limiting and account lockout), scan reports and alert messages for your monitored domains, and a suppression record if you unsubscribe (a one-way hash of your email + the unsubscribe timestamp).

Our scanner fetches only publicly accessible pages (/, /robots.txt, /llms.txt, /sitemap.xml, /openapi.json) of domains you submit. We never scan private networks or authenticated content.

Why we collect it

  • To send you a confirmation email and, later, early-access notifications you explicitly requested.
  • To prevent abuse (rate limiting, mail-bombing protection).
  • To keep an auditable record of your consent (double opt-in: your signup timestamp, IP, and confirmation click).

How long we keep it

  • Unconfirmed waitlist signups are automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • Confirmed subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe, or for up to 12 months — whichever comes first.
  • After you unsubscribe we keep only a minimal suppression record (a one-way hash of your email address + the unsubscribe timestamp), refreshed on access, so we can honor your choice and prevent re-subscription. The raw email address is deleted.
  • Scan cache is kept for 24 hours to serve repeat views. Public scan history is kept per domain up to the most recent 50 records or 12 months, whichever comes first; older rows are deleted automatically.
  • Rate-limit counters expire after 1 hour.
  • Aggregate usage statistics are kept for 90 days in Cloudflare Analytics Engine, then automatically deleted.
  • Monitor account data is kept until you delete your account (available at any time from your panel — it removes your account, monitored domains, scan history, and alerts) or you unsubscribe (which stops all monitoring and leaves only the suppression hash).

Unsubscribing

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Clicking it takes effect immediately and idempotently. You can also email privacy@toolfront.dev to exercise any right below. Domain owners may request removal of their domain's public scan history at the same address; we verify ownership with a DNS TXT or file challenge (instructions by return email) and delete the records within 7 days.

Third-party processors

  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, key-value storage, and aggregate analytics storage (Workers Analytics Engine, 90-day retention) (data processing agreement available).
  • Resend — transactional email delivery for confirmation emails (data processing agreement available).

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where these providers operate. For visitors from the EEA/UK, transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; both processors are certified under the DPF at the time of writing. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone else.

Your rights

Where GDPR, CCPA, CASL, or PIPL applies to you, you may request access to, correction of, portability (export) of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@toolfront.dev. Monitor accounts can also export their data as a JSON file from the panel and delete their account there. We respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority if you believe we processed your data unlawfully. After unsubscribing we retain only a minimal suppression record (email hash + unsubscribe timestamp) to honor your opt-out and prevent re-subscription.

Security

Data in transit is TLS-encrypted. Access to storage is restricted to the production Worker. If you believe you found a security issue, see our vulnerability disclosure policy.

Data breach notification

If a security incident affects personal data we hold, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it (GDPR Art. 33) and, where the incident is likely to result in a high risk to your rights, inform affected users without undue delay by email (GDPR Art. 34). We deliberately keep the data we hold small — an email address, a hashed consent record, the domains you scan or monitor, and hashed passwords for Monitor accounts — which limits the impact of any incident.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where applicable law sets a higher age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided us personal data, contact privacy@toolfront.dev and we will delete it.

Automated scores (no profiling of people)

Readiness scores, grades, and alerts are computed from publicly fetched technical signals of the domain you submit or monitor — file presence, markup, headers. They evaluate a website, not a natural person: no profiling, no automated decisions about an individual (GDPR Art. 22), and no cross-site tracking of you. The scan itself fetches only public pages and never executes the target's scripts.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change; material changes will be announced on the site before taking effect. This is version 1.3 of the policy.

隐私政策

版本 1.3 · 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 2 日 · toolfront.dev

本政策适用于 toolfront.dev（AI 就绪度扫描器）与 monitor.toolfront.dev（Monitor 账号系统），两者由同一处理者按下列相同承诺运营。

我们是谁（个人信息处理者）

ToolFront 由独立开发者 David（所在地：中国上海）运营（下称"我们"）。处理者联系方式：privacy@toolfront.dev。

我们收集什么

  • 电子邮箱——仅当你主动加入候补名单或索取工具蓝图时收集。
  • 你扫描的域名——你在扫描器中输入的域名，如与候补请求一同提交则关联保存。
  • IP 地址与时间戳——用于限流、防滥用，以及（针对候补注册）作为 GDPR 第 7 条要求的同意凭证。
  • 语言偏好——存储在你浏览器的 localStorage（键名 tf-lang）中，用于记住语言选择。这是严格功能性存储；本站不使用 Cookie 或第三方跟踪器。
  • 浏览器本地对比数据——你的修复清单勾选状态与最近一次结果的便利副本（键名 tf-fix:*、tf-last:*）存储于浏览器 localStorage，用于"对比上次扫描"与记住修复进度。这份副本永不离开浏览器，可在浏览器设置中清除（扫描的权威记录见下方公开扫描历史）。
  • 公开扫描历史——当某域名的扫描成功时，该次扫描的记录（域名、分数、等级、逐项检查状态、评分版本与时间）会存储于服务器并公开展示在报告页与排行榜上，以便跨时间比较分数。扫描仅抓取公网可达页面（与爬虫方式相同）：不使用任何凭据，绝不提交表单。通过 robots.txt 声明拒绝扫描的站点永远不会被扫描。IP 地址仅用于限流，绝不与扫描记录一同存储。
  • 聚合用量统计——产品事件计数（如注册、邮箱确认、登录、添加监控）及其粗粒度结果，用于理解注册漏斗、改进产品。这些计数器无法识别你：不包含邮箱、域名、IP 或语言。存储于我们的主机方 Cloudflare Analytics Engine，保留 90 天。
  • Monitor 账号数据——如你在 monitor.toolfront.dev 创建 Monitor 账号：邮箱地址与密码（仅以加盐 PBKDF2 哈希存储，不可还原）、你选择监控的域名、登录时间戳与 IP（用于限流与账号锁定）、你监控域名的扫描报告与告警消息，以及退订抑制记录（邮箱单向哈希 + 退订时间戳）。

扫描器只抓取你提交域名的公开页面（/、/robots.txt、/llms.txt、/sitemap.xml、/openapi.json），绝不扫描私网或需要登录的内容。

为什么收集

  • 向你发送确认邮件，以及后续你明确订阅的产品通知。
  • 防止滥用（限流、防邮件轰炸）。
  • 保留可审计的同意记录（双重确认：注册时间戳、IP、确认点击）。

保留多久

  • 未确认的候补注册：7 天后自动删除。
  • 已确认的订阅：保留至你退订，最长不超过 12 个月（以先到者为准）。
  • 退订后：仅保留最小抑制记录（你邮箱的单向哈希 + 退订时间戳，访问时自动续期），用于执行你的退订选择、防止再次订阅；原始邮箱地址即被删除。
  • 扫描缓存：保留 24 小时用于重复查看。公开扫描历史：每域名保留最近 50 条或 12 个月（以先到者为准），更早记录自动删除。
  • 限流计数器：1 小时后过期。
  • 聚合用量统计：保留于 Cloudflare Analytics Engine 90 天，到期自动删除。
  • Monitor 账号数据：保留至你删除账号（随时可在面板操作——将删除账号、监控域名、扫描历史与告警），或直至你退订（退订即停止全部监控，仅保留抑制哈希）。

退订

我们发出的每封邮件都附带退订链接，点击后立即生效且可重复点击。你也可以发邮件至 privacy@toolfront.dev 行使下述任何权利。域名所有者可在同一邮箱申请移除其域名的公开扫描历史；我们会通过 DNS TXT 或文件挑战验证所有权（验证方法随邮件回复），并在 7 天内删除相关记录。

第三方处理者

  • Cloudflare——托管、DNS、键值存储与聚合分析存储（Workers Analytics Engine，保留 90 天）（已签署数据处理协议）。
  • Resend——确认邮件的事务性投递（已签署数据处理协议）。

数据可能在上述服务商运营所在的美国及其他国家/地区被处理。对来自欧洲经济区/英国的用户，跨境传输依据 EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework 及/或欧盟委员会标准合同条款（SCCs）进行；截至本政策发布，两家服务商均已获得 DPF 认证。我们不出售、不出租、不与任何其他方共享你的个人数据。

你的权利

如 GDPR、CCPA、CASL 或 PIPL 适用于你，你可随时发邮件至 privacy@toolfront.dev 要求访问、更正、导出（可移植性）或删除你的个人数据，我们将在 30 天内响应。Monitor 账号也可在面板中导出你的数据（JSON 文件）并删除账号。如你认为我们非法处理了你的数据，你也有权向当地数据保护监管机构提出投诉。退订后我们仅保留最小抑制记录（邮箱哈希 + 退订时间），用于执行你的退订选择、防止再次订阅。

安全

传输数据经 TLS 加密。存储访问仅限生产 Worker。如发现安全问题，请参阅 漏洞披露政策。

数据泄露通知

若发生影响我们所持有个人数据的安全事件，我们将在知悉后 72 小时内通知相关监管机构（GDPR 第 33 条）；若该事件可能对你的权利造成较高风险，我们将通过邮件及时告知受影响用户（GDPR 第 34 条）。我们刻意将持有的数据保持在最小范围——一个邮箱地址、一条哈希化的同意记录、你扫描或监控的域名，以及 Monitor 账号的哈希化密码——以降低任何事件的潜在影响。

儿童

我们的服务不面向 13 岁以下儿童，或适用法律规定更高年龄时的该更高年龄。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人数据。如你认为儿童向我们提供了个人数据，请联系 privacy@toolfront.dev，我们将予以删除。

自动化评分（不对自然人画像）

就绪度分数、等级与告警均基于你提交或监控域名的公开技术信号计算——文件存在性、标记、响应头。它们评估的是网站而非自然人：不存在画像，不涉及对个人的自动化决策（GDPR 第 22 条），也不对你进行跨站跟踪。扫描本身只抓取公开页面，绝不执行目标网站的脚本。

变更

如实践发生变化我们会更新本页；重大变更将在生效前于站内公告。当前为隐私政策版本 1.3。
